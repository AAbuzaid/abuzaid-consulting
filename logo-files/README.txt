ABUZAID CONSULTING - LOGO FILES
===============================

These are the master logo files. They are made of shapes instead of pixels,
so they stay sharp at any size, from a business card to a large sign.
The lettering is already turned into shapes, so the files look the same on
every computer without installing any fonts.


WHICH FOLDER
------------
pdf/   For printing: business cards, letterheads, brochures, signs.
       Print colours (CMYK). Send these to printers and designers.

svg/   For screens: websites, Word, PowerPoint, Canva, email.
       Screen colours (RGB), the same as the website.


WHICH LOGO
----------
logo-full    The icon above the name and the line
             "LEADERS FOR PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT AND MANAGEMENT, LLC".
             Use it at least 10 cm (4 in) wide so the small line stays readable.

logo-short   The icon beside the name, without the small line.
             For small spaces: business cards, email signatures, website headers.

icon         The head on its own: profile pictures, stamps, small corners.


WHICH COLOUR
------------
navy    For white or light backgrounds. This is the main version.
white   For dark backgrounds and photos. It looks empty on a white screen;
        that is expected.
black   For one-colour printing: black-and-white documents, stamps, engraving.

The CMYK values are matched to the screen colours for standard coated paper
(SWOP). A printer may fine-tune them for their own press; ask for a printed
proof if the exact colour matters.


FONTS (already turned into shapes in these files)
-----
Name:        Roboto Slab SemiBold
Small line:  Inter Medium, in capitals, letter-spaced
Both are free Google Fonts.


TIPS
----
- Resize from a corner so the logo keeps its proportions. Never stretch it.
- Leave empty space around it, at least the height of the "A" in "Abuzaid".
